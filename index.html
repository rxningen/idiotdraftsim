Draft grades & season projection

Grades each team's most-likely drafted roster (from your simulations) using player projections: optimal starting lineups are scored, position groups are compared against the league, and then the full fantasy season is simulated 1,000 times on a generated round-robin schedule (13 unique matchups + a week-1 rematch, top 6 make the playoffs, top 2 seeds get byes). Fantasy schedules vary by league, so records are estimates of team strength, not exact predictions.
